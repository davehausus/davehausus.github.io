## From understanding to experience

Nomo's experience is built around understanding people, the realities of caregiving, and what technology should make possible.

This operating system connects human needs to strategy, principles, creative decisions, communication, motion, interface design, and implementation. It gives us a shared foundation for making decisions, creating coherent experiences, and evolving the product with intention.

Start with the foundations to understand the direction behind the system, or explore a specific area to find the guidance you need.

## Where to begin

If you're new to Nomo's Product Experience OS, begin with the foundations. They establish the purpose and direction behind the decisions that follow.

If you're working on a specific product challenge, go directly to the relevant area and follow its connections to the research, principles, and decisions that inform it.

This is a working system, not a collection of rules set in stone. It evolves as we learn more about people, the product, and the realities of caregiving.
