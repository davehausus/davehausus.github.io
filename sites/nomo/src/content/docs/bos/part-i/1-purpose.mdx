Nomo exists to help people stay connected to the people they care for, even when they cannot be physically present.

Caregiving can create a difficult balance. The person receiving care wants to maintain their independence and privacy. The people who care for them want to know how they are doing and whether they need support.

Nomo exists to help bring these needs closer together.

## The human need

Care does not always happen in the same place.

Families, friends, and caregivers may live apart from the people they support. They cannot always visit, check in, or respond immediately when something changes.

This distance can create uncertainty.

A missed call does not necessarily mean something is wrong. A change in routine does not necessarily indicate a problem. But when people have limited information about someone they care for, it can become difficult to understand what is happening or decide whether support is needed.

The person receiving care faces a different but related concern.

Maintaining independence matters. Support should not automatically mean giving up privacy, control, or the ability to live life on one's own terms.

**The human need is to remain connected and informed without making independence feel conditional on being monitored.**

## Why Nomo exists

Nomo began with a personal experience of caregiving and loss.

Our founder experienced the sudden loss of his father to a stroke. His family had no way to contact or monitor his father's condition or know that he had experienced a medical crisis.

That experience revealed a difficult reality: when people cannot be physically present, they may have limited ways to understand what is happening to someone they care about.

It inspired the creation of Nomo as a way for families and caregivers to check in and communicate with their loved ones remotely.

Nomo Smart Care was created to help address that need.

## The role of Nomo

Nomo connects people through information about daily activity, communication, and alerts.

The system uses sensors and a connected Hub to provide activity information to the mobile app. Caregivers can view information about routines, receive notifications, communicate with the person receiving care, and access the system's assistance features.

These capabilities support a broader human purpose.

They help people remain involved in one another's lives when distance or other circumstances make direct contact difficult.

Nomo is designed without cameras, providing an alternative to continuous visual observation. This reflects the importance of supporting caregiving while respecting the privacy of the person receiving care.

The product is a means of supporting the relationship between people. Its purpose is not defined by the presence of sensors, alerts, or monitoring alone.

## What the purpose establishes

The purpose establishes the human context for the Nomo Product Experience Operating System.

It identifies the needs that subsequent chapters must consider:

- **Independence:** The person receiving care remains an active participant in their own life.
- **Connection:** Caregivers need meaningful ways to remain involved when they cannot be physically present.
- **Reassurance:** People need ways to understand how someone is doing without relying entirely on repeated check-ins.
- **Privacy:** Support should respect the personal space and dignity of the person receiving care.

These needs provide context for future strategic and experience decisions.

They do not determine a specific positioning, interface, interaction pattern, or communication style. Those decisions require their own evidence, principles, and chapter ownership.

## The foundation for what follows

The purpose of Nomo is to support connection and reassurance while respecting the independence of the person receiving care.

This establishes the human reason the product exists.

The chapters that follow will define how Nomo translates that purpose into strategic direction, organizational behavior, and a coherent product experience.

The operating system must preserve this relationship as the product evolves: human needs inform strategy, strategy guides experience decisions, and those decisions shape how Nomo communicates, behaves, and supports people in their everyday lives.
