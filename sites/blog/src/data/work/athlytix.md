## GOAL
Design an iPad app that makes stat-tracking effortless during a fast-paced game. It needed to feel clean and simple for amateurs, while still powerful enough to capture the full story of a game.

## ROLE
I led the product design and brand identity, building the visual system and interaction patterns from scratch.
