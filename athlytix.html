ATHLYTIX

Stats Made Simple for Everyday Athletes

OVERVIEW

Athlytix brings advanced basketball analytics to players, parents, and coaches who want real insights without the complexity of pro-level software. The app lets users create teams and track live game stats such as rebounds, turnovers, assists, made and missed shots, and even shot locations on the court, all from an intuitive, touch-optimized interface.

GOAL

Design an iPad app that makes stat-tracking effortless during a fast-paced game. It needed to feel clean and simple for amateurs, while still powerful enough to capture the full story of a game.

ROLE

I led the product design and brand identity, building the visual system and interaction patterns from scratch.

The Challenge

A fast sport demands fast input

Basketball moves quickly. If a UI forces you to pause and think, you miss plays. The biggest challenge was reducing cognitive load so users could track stats in real time without relying on memory.

A market split between “too simple” and “too complex”

Two main competitors dominated the space: HoopMetrics and TurboStats. Each one sat at a different end of the spectrum.

Both shared a major flaw: uninspired interfaces that slowed users down. With poor visual hierarchy and dense layouts, even simple actions required too much effort.

Athlytix needed to stand out by being both powerful and effortless.

Design Approach

TOUCH-FIRST INTERACTION

The UI was built around quick, thumb-friendly gestures:

  • Large stat buttons for rapid tapping
  • Shot chart interface with instant placement
  • Clear visual feedback to confirm every action

This allowed users to track plays without taking their eyes off the court.

A VISUAL SYSTEM THAT SUPPORTS SPEED

The design focused on reducing noise:

  • Clean typography that’s readable from a distance
  • High-contrast color coding for quick stat recognition
  • Thoughtful spacing that prevents accidental taps

Every screen was optimized for fast decision-making.

BRAND IDENTITY THAT REFLECTS MOTION + PRECISION

I created a brand that feels athletic but modern — energetic yet minimal. The identity supports the product without overpowering it, letting the data remain the hero.

Outcome

The result is an app that gives everyday athletes and coaches the analytical tools typically reserved for professionals, without overwhelming them.

Athlytix proves that powerful tools don’t need complex interfaces. With the right hierarchy, motion, and clarity, even advanced stat tracking becomes simple.

Reflection

Designing for speed teaches you to remove friction everywhere:

  • Fewer taps
  • Clearer paths
  • Cleaner visuals
  • Smarter defaults

Athlytix became a project that reinforced one of my core design beliefs: if the interface slows you down, it’s broken.
