About me

I’m David de Jesus, a Senior UI & Motion Designer specializing in interactive motion systems and Rive-based animation for digital products.

Since 2009, I’ve worked across web, mobile, and large-screen interfaces, helping teams turn static UI into clear, responsive, and expressive product experiences. My work lives at the intersection of interface design, motion systems, and interaction logic, where animation is not decoration, but a functional layer of how a product communicates.

I focus on designing motion that clarifies workflows, explains state, guides attention, and improves usability. Whether I’m building Rive state machines, defining motion systems for scalable products, or prototyping complex interactions, I approach motion with a product-first, system-driven mindset.

My process blends UI design, system thinking, and animation craft. I pay close attention to timing, transitions, and feedback, ensuring motion decisions support both the user experience and the underlying design system. I work closely with product managers and engineers to deliver solutions that are production-ready and built to scale.

Based in Guadalajara, Mexico, I collaborate with teams and clients worldwide. In addition to client work, I’ve spoken at conferences and led workshops on UI, UX, and prototyping, sharing practical experience with students and designers entering the field.

I see motion as a design language. When used with intent, it reduces complexity, reinforces usability, and gives digital products a clear, human personality.

What I Work On
  • Product UI and motion design
  • Rive animations and State Machines
  • Motion systems for scalable products
  • Interactive prototypes for web and mobile applications
