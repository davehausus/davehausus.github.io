About me

I’m a UI & Motion Designer specializing in Rive animation services, Rive State Machine development, and Lottie animation for apps. Since 2009, I’ve been focused on turning static interfaces into dynamic product experiences through thoughtful motion and interaction design.

I create animations that do more than look good, they clarify workflows, guide attention, and make products feel more responsive and human. Whether I’m building Rive State Machine logic, creating Rive Data Bind examples, or developing scalable micro-interactions for mobile apps, I approach motion with a product-driven mindset.

My work blends UI design, prototyping, and motion systems with a detail-obsessed approach where every transition, timing curve, and interaction is intentional. I believe motion is a real design language, one that simplifies complexity, supports usability, and gives products a memorable personality.
